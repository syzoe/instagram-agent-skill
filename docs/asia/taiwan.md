Two meaning traps. (1) In Taiwan, **社群** means *social media*, as in 社群平台 and 「台灣社群通訊行為調查」 (C). It does not mean a community group. (2) **發票** in Taiwan means the 統一發票 / 電子發票 receipt system, not a mainland-style invoice (U). Script trap: never output Cantonese characters (嘅, 咗, 啲, 冇) or Simplified characters.

### 3b. Register, mixing and what reads fake

- **English mixing** (INFERENCE): feature names stay in English (IG, Reels, Threads, Live, CP值, OK, vlog); everyday verbs stay Chinese. Full English sentences in a Taiwanese caption read as an imported template.
- **Particles** (INFERENCE): 欸, 啦, 喔, 齁, 吧, 耶 soften tone. One or two per caption sounds natural; one per line sounds like a parody. Taiwan spells it 喔, not 哦.
- **注音文 / homophones**: der (= 的) is a known net form (國家教育研究院 e-paper, [NAER](https://epaper.naer.edu.tw/storage/upfiles/edm_105_2459_pdf_0.pdf), date UNVERIFIED). Bopomofo single letters (ㄅ, ㄇ) and der now read as playful or dated (INFERENCE).
- **Slang ages fast**: 94狂 was a 2016 網路溫度計 top-slang pick ([數位時代](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/55587/internet-language), summary only). 超派 (from 台語, "超兇") went viral in Dec 2023 ([中時](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20231225003356-260404), 2023-12-25). Old slang used today signals someone trying too hard (INFERENCE).
- **Reads fake / try-hard** (INFERENCE): mainland net-speak (yyds, 絕絕子, 打call, 家人們, 寶子們, 集美, 衝鴨), Douyin live-sell openers, 您 throughout a casual post, a stack of slang words (傻眼 + 超派 + der + 笑死 in one caption), and emoji after every clause.
- **Threads tone that works**: short, conversational, self-mocking, replies inside other people's threads (foodNEXT, 2026-01-03).

## 4. Hook and caption clichés

Taiwan's "content farm" (農場文) headline formulas, as listed by 台灣達人秀 TTshow on [LINE TODAY](https://today.line.me/tw/v2/article/yGgzaB) (2021-01-09). Readers complain that the headline promises more than the content delivers:
「原來ＸＸ要這樣用！以前都不知道」「太神了！不看會後悔！」「一定要知道的三件事」(numbered titles)「千萬別ＸＸＸ！」「知道真相後悔都來不及了！」「這一定要收藏／分享！」「看完結局我哭了！」「傻眼了！／無語了！」

| Tired hook / CTA | Why it fails | Use instead (INFERENCE unless noted) |
|---|---|---|
| 90%的人都不知道… / 以前都不知道 | Same family as the TTshow farm formula | A specific claim from your own experience: 「我開店第三年才發現，退貨率高是因為尺寸表」 |
| 一定要知道的X件事 / X個秘訣 | TTshow numbered-title formula | Name the thing and the payoff: 「蝦皮運費從 60 降到 38 的設定」 |
| 必收藏 / 一定要收藏 / 快分享 | TTshow 「這一定要收藏／分享」 | Earn the save: put the checklist on the last slide and say 「存起來下次用」 once, at the end |
| 懶人包 as the hook | Overused label (INFERENCE) | Fine as a carousel *type*, weak as the first line |
| 太神了 / 傻眼了 / 看完我哭了 | TTshow list | Show the reaction on camera instead of announcing it |
| 「留言＋1 我私訊你」 / 「留言『要』傳連結」 | Meta demotes posts that ask for comments, tags or votes on Facebook ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/); Instagram not named on that page) | A keyword DM is OK when it delivers something real; say what they get |
| Fake 心得, hired comments, undisclosed 業配 on Threads | Named as the main Threads 地雷 ([INSIDE](https://www.inside.com.tw/article/36098-threads-marketing), date UNVERIFIED, summary only). A brand Threads account caused an uproar when its agency said it was 「操作」 ([udn](https://udn.com/news/story/120911/9298084), date UNVERIFIED) | A real 小編 voice that discloses partnerships plainly |
| Reposted or aggregated clips | Instagram is cutting recommendations for aggregator accounts (2024 and 2026 updates, [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/) 2026-04-30, summary only) | Original footage, local detail (place names, MRT stations, NT$ prices) |

**Does Taiwan law require online prices to be shown publicly?** No rule found that requires a public price on a social post or bans 「私訊問價」. Treat this as UNVERIFIED, not settled. What the law does say:
- 消費者保護法 **§18**: for distance sales (通訊交易), the 企業經營者 must give the consumer the 對價 (price), payment and delivery terms, and so on, in clear text; for internet sales, in a form the buyer can view and save. This is disclosure to the buyer, not public posting. **§19**: 7-day no-reason cancellation for distance sales. [law.moj J0170001](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0170001) (last amended 2015-06-17).
- 商品標示法 **§6** lists mandatory label items and does not include price ([J0080011](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0080011), 2022-05-18).
- 零售業等網路交易定型化契約應記載及不得記載事項: price shown on the product page is part of the contract, and buyers must be able to confirm the price before ordering (2016 MOEA version, [6laws](https://6laws.net/6law/law3/零售業等網路交易定型化契約應記載及不得記載事項.htm)). A 數位發展部 revision draft was reported ([udn](https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9563948)); final status UNVERIFIED. Reported as not covering individual sellers.
- 公平交易法 **§21**: price is listed among the matters that must not be false or misleading.
- INFERENCE: post the price publicly. It builds trust and avoids accusations of quoting different prices to different people.

## 8. Currency, numbers, dates

- **Currency**: the official name is **新臺幣** (中央銀行發行新臺幣辦法, 2000-01-26, summary only). **新台幣** is the common variant. In copy: `NT$1,280`, `1,280元`, and on IG often `$1,280` (INFERENCE). Write 元; say 塊 (「一千兩百八十塊」, INFERENCE). Use 台幣 only when contrasting with another currency (INFERENCE). The ISO code TWD is UNVERIFIED here.
- **Big numbers** group by 萬 (10⁴) and 億 (10⁸), never by thousands: 1.2萬 = 12,000; 3,000萬 = 30 million; 1.5億 = 150 million. Official text uses this, e.g. 「新臺幣七億五千萬元」 (J0150021). Arabic numerals take a comma every three digits: 「超過 1,000 名粉絲」 (IG help). 千 is fine below 萬 (「三千元」).
- **Discounts** (UNVERIFIED, standard usage): 8折 / 打八折 = 20% off; 85折 = 15% off; 「買一送一」; 「滿千折百」. Never write "20折" for 20% off.
- **Dates**: `2026/11/3` or `11/3（二）`; weekday in brackets (一)…(日) as in the 人事總處 tables. 民國 years appear in government text: 115年 = 2026, 116年 = 2027. Do not use 2026年11月3日 with a Western weekday such as "Tuesday" (INFERENCE).
