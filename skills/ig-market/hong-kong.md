# Hong Kong

Read with `chinese.md`. The sourced research behind every line here, with
dates and links, is `docs/asia/hong-kong.md` in the repo. UNVERIFIED marks
what no published source confirms. This is not legal advice.

## Where people are

- Instagram reaches 54.8% of the population and is the main brand and KOL
  channel (DataReportal, Oct 2025). YouTube (84.4%) and Facebook (63.6%) are
  bigger.
- **WhatsApp is the shop counter.** 71% contact businesses on it every week
  (Meta/Kantar 2024). Bookings, orders and service go there.
- Xiaohongshu is where people research products before buying (about 62%
  use it, 2026 survey). Threads is text-first gossip and workplace talk.
- **TikTok left Hong Kong in 2020.** Never plan Hong Kong TikTok content.
- Path: discover on IG or Xiaohongshu, check reviews, then ask or order on
  WhatsApp or by IG DM.

## Register: pick one per piece

| Register | Looks like | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Standard written Chinese (書面語) | 的, 了, 在, 不, 沒有 | Statements, apologies, terms, lucky-draw rules, any health or regulated claim |
| Written Chinese with Hong Kong words | 質素, 上載, 著數, 減價, a little English (book位), the odd 嘅 | Brand captions, carousel text, headlines |
| Cantonese (粵語) | 嘅, 咗, 喺, 唔, 冇, 啲, 嘢, 佢哋 | Reel voiceover, Stories, replies, DMs, WhatsApp |

Do not switch register mid-sentence. A common brand pattern is a written-
Chinese caption with one Cantonese punchline line (UNVERIFIED). English sits
inside Cantonese unchanged and lowercase: book咗位, send個file俾你. Keep
everyday English (OK, book, confirm, like, share, follow, deadline). Translate
abstract slide-deck English (mindset, journey, empower) into plain Cantonese.

## Cantonese voiceover

Reels are spoken in Cantonese. A script reads as translated from Mandarin
when only the characters were swapped and the word order stayed:

| Cantonese | Mandarin order (wrong) |
|---|---|
| 你行先, 食咗飯先 (先 after the verb) | 你先走, 先吃飯 |
| 畀本書我 (thing, then person) | 畀我本書 |
| 佢高過我 | 佢比我高 |

Particles each have a job. One per line at most, and only where it means
something: 喎 (realising, reminding), 囉 (the obvious conclusion), 㗎 (stating
a fact firmly), 啫 / 咋 (only, playing down: 十蚊啫), 啦 (softening a request:
快啲book啦), 咩 (surprise), 添 (and also: 仲有贈品添).

Tells of a machine-written Hong Kong script: Taiwanese particles (喔, 唷, 欸,
齁), mainland buzzwords (賦能, 內卷, 種草, 打工人, 爆款), written verbs in
speech (進行, 能夠), a particle on every clause, and Taiwan vocabulary (線上,
貼文, 專案).

Reel on-screen text: either the voiceover's Cantonese, or a short written-
Chinese headline card. Pick one per Reel and do not mix inside a card
(inference, no source).

## Money and numbers

- A bare `$` means HK$ in Hong Kong copy. Write $99 or HK$99. Never NT$, ¥
  or RMB unless that is the currency.
- 蚊 only follows a number (一百蚊, 十蚊啫). 元 is formal.
- **八折 means you pay 80%** (20% off). 六折 means you pay 60%. Never write
  "20% off" as 二折, which means paying 20%.
- Big numbers in 萬 and 億 ($1.2萬 or $12,000). Dates day first: 1月1日, or
  DD/MM, never MM/DD.

## Comments, replies, DMs

- Reply in the commenter's language and register: Cantonese to Cantonese,
  English to English. Keep it short.
- 價錢PM / PM我 / inbox are common. But hidden prices plus "move to
  WhatsApp" is also the scam pattern: Instagram was 25% of 11,449 online
  shopping scams in Jan-Nov 2025 (police figures). Default: show a price or a
  range in the post, list one official WhatsApp number in the bio, and never
  move a buyer to an unlisted number.
- Once a DM quotes a price, the seller's identity, address, full price and
  delivery charge should be available to the buyer (Trade Descriptions
  Ordinance, inference that it covers DMs).
- Saving a commenter's or buyer's phone number to send promotions is direct
  marketing: give notice and get consent first (Privacy Ordinance).
- Complaints: acknowledge once in public, then move details to DM. Formal
  apologies go in written Chinese, never slang.
- On IG write `link喺bio` / `詳情睇bio`, not 入blog睇.

## Claims and rules

| Rule | What it means for a post |
|---|---|
| Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap 362), Customs | 全港最平 needs evidence. 原價$X 而家$Y: the original price must really have been charged. 全場半價 must be true for every item. 低至X折 needs "selected items" if few items get it. No hidden charges. Unlabelled advertorials, staff posing as customers and fake 朋友仔 reviews are misleading omissions. Max $500,000 and 5 years |
| Undesirable Medical Advertisements Ordinance (Cap 231), Dept of Health | No ad for treating the listed diseases. For products you eat or drink (not ordinary food): no claims about breast lumps, the genitourinary system or hormone regulation. Blood sugar, blood pressure and blood lipid claims only in the set wording (e.g. 此產品或有助於穩定血糖。) plus the set disclaimer. 排毒 and 調節荷爾蒙 hooks hit this |
| Proprietary Chinese medicine (Cap 549) | Must be registered before sale. Ads also fall under Cap 231 |
| Alcohol | No supply to under-18s, online included. Online sellers must show the set notice and get an age declaration |
| Tobacco (Cap 371) | No smoking-product ad on the internet. A visible tobacco brand counts. E-cigarettes and heated tobacco cannot be promoted at all |
| Lucky draws (Gambling Ordinance) | Any prize given by chance, including a random pick from commenters, needs a Trade Promotion Competition Licence: no cash prizes, no entry fee, the licence number on every post, results in two newspapers within 10 days. Max $5,000,000 and 7 years. Instagram also does not recommend giveaway posts to non-followers |
| Sponsored posts | No influencer-specific law. An advertorial must be clear it is an ad (Cap 362): use Instagram's paid-partnership label. Paid investment tips without an SFC licence have led to prison |

## Hooks

Low-effort on Hong Kong IG (inference): 你一定唔知…, 原來…, 竟然…, 全港首間,
隱世, 必食 / 必去, 中伏？ as bait, 超正🔥🔥🔥. What works: things people can
check (price, district, MTR exit, queue time, portion), footage that looks
like a customer shot it, and holding one register the whole way.
