# Taiwan

Read with `chinese.md`. The sourced research behind every line here, with
dates and links, is `docs/asia/taiwan.md` in the repo. UNVERIFIED marks what
no published source confirms. This is not legal advice.

## Where people are

- Facebook is still the single most-used platform (51% of internet users name
  it first), Instagram second (21%), Threads third (4.6%) (TWNIC 2025).
- **By age:** 78% of 18-24s use Instagram and 44% use Threads. Facebook peaks
  at 45-54 (資策會 MIC, Q4 2024). Instagram is the under-35 channel.
- **LINE is where the sale closes.** 90% of internet users message on it, and
  3.4M businesses run LINE Official Accounts for coupons, points, bookings and
  service. 「加 LINE 好友領券」 is a normal ask in Taiwan, not spam.
- **Threads (「脆」)** is text conversation: short, casual, self-mocking brand
  voices (「小編」) and brands replying under other people's threads.
- Path: discover on IG, talk on Threads, buy on an e-commerce platform or
  close on LINE.

## Register

- 台灣正體 only. Never Simplified characters, never Cantonese characters
  (嘅, 咗, 啲, 冇).
- Use 你 on IG and Threads. 您 for formal service, complaints, older buyers
  and B2B.
- English for feature names and a few loanwords (IG, Reels, Threads, Live,
  CP值, OK, vlog). A whole English sentence reads as an imported template.
- Particles soften tone: 欸, 啦, 喔 (not 哦), 齁, 吧. One or two per caption.
  One per line reads as parody.
- Slang ages fast. Old net slang (94狂, der, ㄅ) reads as trying too hard;
  so does stacking 傻眼 + 超派 + 笑死 in one caption.
- Reads fake: mainland net-speak (yyds, 絕絕子, 家人們, 寶子們, 集美, 衝鴨),
  Douyin live-selling openers, emoji after every clause.

## Money, numbers, dates

- `NT$1,280`, `1,280元`, or `$1,280` on IG. Write 元, say 塊. 新台幣 is fine;
  台幣 only when contrasting with another currency.
- Big numbers in 萬 and 億: 1.2萬 = 12,000, 3,000萬 = 30 million.
- **8折 means 20% off** (you pay 80%), 85折 means 15% off. Also 買一送一,
  滿千折百. Never write 20折 for 20% off.
- Dates: `11/3（二）` or `2026/11/3`. Government text uses 民國 years:
  115年 = 2026.

## Comments, replies, DMs

- 哈哈, XD and 笑死 are normal. 233 and hhh read as mainland.
- 謝謝 for everyday thanks, 感謝支持 to the community, 不好意思 to open an
  apology or correction.
- 「+1」 means "I'll take one" in Facebook group-buys.
- 私訊問價 (price by DM only) is common and often criticised. No law found
  requiring a public price on a post (UNVERIFIED), but the Consumer
  Protection Act (§18) requires the price and terms in writing to the buyer
  for online sales, and the Fair Trade Act (§21) bans misleading prices.
  Default: put the price in the post.
- Collecting names, phones or addresses in DMs, forms or giveaways: say who
  you are, why, and for how long; use them only for that; give an opt-out
  with the first marketing message (Personal Data Protection Act §8, §20).

## Claims and rules

| Rule | What it means for a post |
|---|---|
| Sponsored posts (Fair Trade Commission endorsement guideline; Fair Trade Act §21) | Any paid or gifted relationship, including free product, must be disclosed in the post. The endorser must be a real user. Endorsers can be jointly liable; businesses face NT$50,000-25,000,000. Also use Instagram's 品牌合作標籤. No wording is prescribed: 「#業配」「本篇與○○合作」 are common |
| Food (食品安全衛生管理法 §28) | No false or exaggerated claims, and **never a medical effect**: no preventing, improving or treating any disease or symptom, no claims to change body function. Medical claims: NT$600,000-5,000,000, fined per post |
| Health food (健康食品管理法) | Only licensed products may be called 健康食品, and only within the approved claim |
| Cosmetics and skincare (化粧品衛生安全管理法 §10) | No false or exaggerated claims, no medical effect (anything that treats or prevents a condition). Medical claims: NT$600,000-5,000,000 |
| Medical and aesthetic (醫療法 §84, §87) | **Only medical institutions may advertise medical services**, aesthetic treatments included. A post that implies a medical service counts as a medical ad |
| Alcohol (菸酒管理法 §37) | Every alcohol post must show 「禁止酒駕」 and 「飲酒過量，有害健康」 (or another warning). No targeting minors, no health claims |
| Giveaways (Fair Trade Act §23) | Purchase-linked draws: top prize at most NT$5,000,000. Prizes over NT$20,000 are taxed at 10% (residents) |

For a regulated product, write the experience, not a medical effect:
「我自己敷了兩週，早上上妝比較服貼」, never 「治療痘痘」 for a cosmetic or
「降血糖」 for food. Which non-medical claims are safe is set out in the
regulators' annexes: check them before a results claim, and tell the user.

## Hooks

Taiwan's content-farm headline formulas, now read as clickbait: 原來ＸＸ要這樣用,
太神了, 不看會後悔, 一定要知道的三件事, 千萬別ＸＸ, 這一定要收藏, 看完我哭了,
傻眼了. Also tired: 90%的人都不知道, 懶人包 as the first line, 必收藏.

What works instead: a specific claim from your own experience
(「我開店第三年才發現，退貨率高是因為尺寸表」), a named payoff
(「蝦皮運費從 60 降到 38 的設定」), local detail (place names, MRT stations,
NT$ prices), original footage, and partnerships disclosed plainly.
